our story

Rooted in craft,
served with soul

SAVOR began as a small corner bistro with a simple belief: great food brings people together. Today, we continue that tradition with a focus on seasonal ingredients, open-fire cooking, and genuine hospitality.

Our kitchen is led by Chef Maya, whose global travels inspire a menu that's both comforting and adventurous. Every plate is an invitation to slow down and savor.
